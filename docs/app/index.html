◆ forge remote

Your laptop, in your pocket.

Send Forge tasks, approve what it wants to do, and see your screen — from anywhere.

  1. On your laptop, open Forge and type /remote
  2. Scan the QR code it shows

On iPhone you can also just point the Camera app at the code and tap the link it shows.

End-to-end encrypted: only your laptop and this phone hold the key. The relay in between only ever sees scrambled bytes.
